TILT IT.

Tilt your phone to roll the steel ball through the maze into the green goal. Miss a turn and it drops through a hole back to the start.

Hold the phone the way you like before tapping Start. That angle counts as level. No sensor? Hold the board and drag toward where you want the ball to roll, or use the arrow keys.
